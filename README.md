# Solar Sales Analysis

An end-to-end sales analytics project using **Python, MySQL, and Power BI** to analyse solar equipment distribution data and generate actionable business insights.

## Project Objective

To identify revenue trends, supplier performance, customer behaviour, and delivery patterns through data analysis and interactive dashboards, supporting data-driven business decisions.

## Tech Stack

- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Visualization:** Power BI
- **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled duplicate records and missing values.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Analysed sales trends, products, suppliers, customer types, discounts, and cities.
3. **SQL Analysis:** Answered 11 business questions covering sales performance, operations, and customer insights.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Built a Power BI dashboard to visualise sales and operational performance.

## Measurable Project Outcomes

- Cleaned and analysed a **3,900-row synthetic dataset**.
- Developed SQL analysis for **11 business questions**.
- Identified that Waaree and Adani Solar together contributed approximately **85% of reported supplier revenue**.
- Found that express delivery orders had an approximately **15% higher average order value** than standard deliveries.
- Developed a Power BI dashboard to support sales and operational analysis.

## Key Analysis Areas

- Sales performance by customer type and quarter
- Supplier revenue and market share
- Product demand and discount patterns
- Shipping methods and delivery performance
- Installation activity by city
- Customer segmentation and revenue contribution

## Note

This project uses a **synthetic solar operations dataset** for analytical and portfolio purposes. The findings reflect the dataset and its assumptions and should not be interpreted as verified real-world company performance.
